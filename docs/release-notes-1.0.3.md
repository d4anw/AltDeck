## RoDeck 1.0.3

- **Search as you type everywhere:** results show up while you type, no Enter needed.
- **Picking a game:** the game picker shows games straight away, before you type anything. The separate "Browse games" is gone; it's all in one place now.
- **Server browser:** every server row has its own **Select** button, and the fps column is gone.
- **Avatar size:** one slider for size instead of several.
- **Trade ads:**
  - Each account has its **own ad**. Switching accounts switches the ad page with it, and the Offer picker always shows the limiteds of the account you're on.
  - One ad posts at a time. Press **Start** on another account to move posting there.
  - Faces Roblox turned into dynamic heads (like Green Starry Sight) can be offered again. RoDeck posts them under the ID Rolimons lists them as and shows their Rolimons value.
  - The last result says which items were left out of the ad and why (not on Rolimons, on trade hold, no longer owned).
  - When Rolimons says to wait, RoDeck checks again every 30 seconds and posts the moment it's allowed.
  - The item picker opens right under the slot you clicked.
- **Private servers:** **Add people** suggests your friends as you type.
- **Client window previews:** a preview that comes out blank gets a second try.
- **Client windows:** windows you bring up with **Show**, **Show all** or **Tile** stay up instead of being minimized again.
- **Tile on launch:** a new setting (Settings) that tiles your windows in a grid once a multi-launch is up.
